What does a keyterm list
do to this call?

Gnani gives you 200 keyterm slots. Your glossary is larger. LexiForge measures what a candidate list does on the same audio — and proves whether it helped or hurt.
glossary→ select list→ baseline · selected · mismatched · hard-negative · oracle · static→ Keyterm Health Report

The gap

Vendor benchmarks show relevant keyterms help on public clips. They do not tell a customer whether a list helps on their own noisy, code-mixed calls — or whether it quietly degrades ordinary recognition.

What we measure

A — critical terms: brand, product, scheme, must-have. B — all-real-term guardrail: keyterms must not wreck words the model already knows.

Why this matters to Gnani

  • Gnani sells the 200 slots; LexiForge measures what a list does on the customer's own audio.
  • It is a POC accelerator and a glossary guardrail, not a competing ASR.
  • Deployment alongside the customer's stack is a roadmap item; the pilot ran locally.
  • Currently synthetic and internal-only; real Hinglish audio is the next step.
